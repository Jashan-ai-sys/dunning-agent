Step 1 — authorise once

Cards must be recurring-eligible. A plain 4111 1111 1111 1111 is rejected with "This card is not eligible for recurring payments".

  1. Try the Mastercard test card 5104 0155 5555 5558, any future expiry, any CVV.
  2. If cards keep refusing, switch to EMandate or UPI in the checkout’s left-hand menu — those authorise without a card.
  3. Authorisation itself should succeed. We fail the charge afterwards.

Step 2 — fail the charge, on demand

Do not fight with OTPs. Razorpay test mode can fire a charge whenever you want and let you pick the outcome:

  1. Razorpay Dashboard → Subscriptions → open this subscription.
  2. Click “Charge this now”.
  3. Choose failure when it asks for the result.

That fires a genuine payment.failed with a real invoice_id, moves the subscription to pending, and opens a recovery case by itself — no timing games, repeatable for a retake.
